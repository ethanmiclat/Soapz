Self-service wash

How it works

Load and pay

Pick a washer that fits your load, add your soap, then pay at the machine with coins or a card.

Wash and dry

A wash takes about 30 minutes. Move your load to a dryer and it will be finished in about another 30.

Fold and go

Use any of the folding tables, free of charge. Carts are by the door if you need help carrying out.

What you get at no extra cost

Everything below is included with any wash. You only ever pay for the machines.

  • Free Wi-Fi, seating, and a clean restroom
  • Detergent and dryer sheets available from the vending wall
