How drop-off works

There is nothing to book ahead and no form to fill in. Bring your laundry in and we will take it from there.

Drop it off

Bring your laundry to the counter in any bag or basket. We weigh it, write you a ticket, and give you a pickup time.

We wash and fold

We sort by colour and fabric, wash and dry each order on its own, then fold everything by hand.

Pick it up

Collect your order at the counter, bagged and ready. Pay when you pick up, by cash or card.

What is included

One price covers the whole job. There are no separate charges for soap, drying or folding.

  • Sorted by colour and fabric before anything goes in
  • Washed on its own, never mixed with another customer's order
  • Detergent and dryer sheets included, or bring your own if you prefer
  • Folded by hand and bagged, ready to put straight in the drawer
  • Tell us about a stain or a delicate item and we will treat it separately

What it costs
